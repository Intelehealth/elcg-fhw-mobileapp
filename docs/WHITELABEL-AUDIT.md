# Consolidated Regression Report — eZazi / eLCG Nepal White-Label Rebuild

**Scope:** working tree (Phases 1–5, Phase 5 uncommitted) vs two baselines.
**Refs:** `HEAD` = 4579f67 · Nepal baseline `bb21866` (dev_kaveri_nepal_sprint_46 tip) · eZazi baseline `origin/development_master` = 5cfe698.

**Lineage, established before anything else:** `git merge-base HEAD bb21866` returns `bb21866` — it is a direct ancestor, and `bb21866..HEAD` is exactly the five rebuild commits (087507f, c609ce9, fae89d8, aa0c99c, 4579f67). `origin/development_master` is **not** an ancestor (merge-base 94a98d6); it is a divergent sibling product baseline. Every diff against bb21866 is the rebuild's exact footprint; every diff against development_master mixes rebuild work with a full Nepal sprint. Both directions are reported below, kept apart, because the fix owners differ.

---

## 1. VERDICT

The rebuild's own mechanical work is sound and I would proceed from it: the 362-file Phase-3 dissolve lost nothing (719 files in `c609ce9`, 719 in `fae89d8`, zero blobs dropped, exactly 9 name collisions handled by the 9 `*_ezazi.xml` renames), there are zero duplicate resource definitions in any source set, zero new dangling references for either flavour with the two flavours' unresolved sets identical name-for-name, all 30 endpoint keys resolve to the intended hosts, every RTDB node path that matters is byte-identical to its baseline, and the rebuild's total Java footprint is 8 files and roughly 20 lines. What is **not** safe to ship from is the state those 20 lines put the two products in. Six blockers stand between here and a release: two of them break a build (`app/src/ezaziDefault/res/values*/strings.xml` are untracked; the staged index still points ezaziDefault's adaptive icons at a name the same commit deletes), two of them change persisted data on a live OpenMRS instance and introduce a new crash path (the `cityvillage` and `REGISTRATION_NUMBER` shape change that follows from Nepal crossing the flavour gate), and two of them ship the wrong brand or the wrong backend (a `:klivekit` string shadow that defeats the entire app-name split on Hindi and Odia devices for **both** flavours, and Nepal's move onto Firebase project `elcg-402e7`, which invalidates every FCM token, relocates Crashlytics and moves the force-update kill switch to a different console). Separately, the eZazi India build still renders Government-of-Nepal artwork on splash, login and home — that one predates the rebuild and is not its doing, but it is the most visible brand defect in the tree and this rebuild is the change that makes `ezaziDefault` a real product line rather than a label. Proceed, but do not commit as staged and do not ship either flavour until §2 is cleared.
